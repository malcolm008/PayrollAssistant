Managing payroll periods ensures your team processes salaries, reviews inputs, and closes monthly cycles smoothly. You can easily view active dates, switch between months, and roll over into the next pay period using the Payroll Period manager.

## Understanding the Monthly Cycle

Every payroll period follows a structured three-step lifecycle to keep your financial records accurate and organized.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select period">
Choose the specific month you want to process from the period calendar.
</Step>
<Step title="Process payroll">
Review time and attendance, calculate wages, and verify inputs before the cut-off date.
</Step>
<Step title="Roll over">
Close the current active month and automatically open the next month in the sequence.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Navigating Payroll Periods

The dashboard provides a clear overview of your current active period, including start and end dates, total days, and a real-time progress bar. 

### Key Dates to Watch

Each active period tracks three critical milestones:

| Date Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Period Start** | The official first day of the payroll cycle. |
| **Input Cut-off** | The deadline for submitting adjustments, overtime, or deductions. |
| **Payment Date** | The scheduled day funds are disbursed to employees. |

> [!TIP]
> Keep an eye on the progress bar in your dashboard. It automatically highlights when a period has ended so you know exactly when it is time to perform a rollover.

## Changing the Active Period

If you need to view or work on a different month, you can change your active period at any time.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the period selector">
Click the **Change Period** button on your active payroll card.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a year and month">
Use the arrow buttons to navigate between years, then select the target month from the grid.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm your selection">
Review the selected date range and click **Set Active Period** to switch.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!WARNING]
> Periods marked as **Locked** or **Closed** cannot be activated directly. Contact your system administrator if you need to reopen a closed period.

## Rolling Over to a New Period

Once you have completed payroll for the current month, rolling over transitions your system to the next cycle.

<Steps>
<Step title="Initiate rollover">
Click the **Roll Over** button on the active payroll card.
</Step>
<Step title="Review the transition">
Check the confirmation modal to ensure you are closing the correct month and opening the expected next month.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Click **Confirm Rollover** to close the current period and activate the next one.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!NOTE]
> The system will prevent a rollover if the next period is already locked or closed, or if you attempt to roll over before the current active period has officially ended.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="What happens to past payroll data when I roll over?">
Past payroll periods are marked as **Closed** and saved in your history. You can still view their details, but you cannot make edits without reopening them.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Can I skip ahead by more than one month?">
No. Moving forward skips months and can disrupt your payroll sequence. Use the standard monthly rollover process to progress sequentially.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="What should I do if a period is locked?">
Locked periods are protected against accidental changes. You will need to reach out to your system administrator to unlock or modify locked historical data.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
