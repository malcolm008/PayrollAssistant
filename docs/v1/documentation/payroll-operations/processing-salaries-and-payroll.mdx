Manage your monthly payroll operations, calculate salaries, and review allowances, deductions, and net payouts in the Payroll Workspace.

The Payroll module helps you streamline salary calculations, manage statutory deductions like PAYE and pension, and run monthly payroll efficiently.

## Getting Started with Payroll

Before processing salaries for the month, you need to register your employees and ensure that allowances and deductions are properly configured.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the payroll period">
Click the period button in the top bar (for example, **August 2026**) to choose the active month you want to view or process.
</Step>
<Step title="Register employees">
Click **Register Employees** to select staff members from the employee directory who should be included in the current payroll run.
</Step>
<Step title="Configure components">
Review and update employee allowances (housing, transport) and deductions (PAYE, pension, health) using the workspace tabs.
</Step>
<Step title="Process payroll">
Once you have verified all details, click **Process Payroll** to calculate totals and finalize the run.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Payroll Components

The payroll system calculates employee take-home pay by combining basic salaries with additional components and subtracting applicable deductions.

| Component Type | Description | Examples |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| **Basic Salary** | The base agreed compensation for the employee. | Fixed monthly salary |
| **Allowances** | Additional financial benefits added to the gross pay. | Housing, transport, and special allowances |
| **Deductions** | Statutory and voluntary amounts subtracted from gross pay. | PAYE, pension contributions, and health insurance |
| **Overtime** | Compensation for extra hours worked beyond standard schedules. | Logged overtime hours and rates |

> [!TIP]
> You can track the status of individual employee records as they move from **Draft** to **Processed** and finally to **Paid**.

## Payroll Processing Workflow

The payroll processing lifecycle ensures accuracy from initial employee selection to final disbursement.

```flowchart TD
    A[Select Period] --> B["Register Employees"]
    B --> C["Configure Allowances and Deductions"]
    C --> D["Review Gross and Net Summaries"]
    D --> E["Process Payroll"]
    E --> F[History and Payslips]
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="How do I add a new allowance?">
Navigate to the **Allowances** tab within the Payroll Workspace, select the employee, and add the specific allowance type and amount.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Can I remove an employee after registering them?">
Yes. You can unregister an employee by clicking the remove icon next to their name in the registration view before processing the payroll.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Where can I view past payroll runs?">
Switch to the **History** tab in the workspace to review summaries, totals, and processing dates for previous months.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
