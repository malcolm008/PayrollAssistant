Managing employee compensation, reviewing individual earnings, and tracking historical payouts are essential parts of running a smooth payroll operation. This guide walks you through navigating payroll workspaces, inspecting individual payslips, and monitoring past payroll records.

## Navigating Payroll Workspaces

The payroll system organizes information into specialized tabs and workspaces. When you open a payroll period, you can easily switch between different aspects of your financial processing using the top navigation bar.

| Workspace Tab | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Overview**  | Summary of total net pay, employee count, and quick access to the payroll register. |
| **Allowances** | Manage earnings components such as housing, transport, and custom additions. |
| **Deductions** | Review statutory items like taxes, pensions, health insurance, and other deductions. |
| **Overtime**  | Track overtime hours, rates, and additional calculated pay. |
| **Payslips**  | Inspect, preview, and download individual employee payslips. |
| **History**   | Review past payroll periods, their statuses, totals, and processing dates. |

> [!TIP]
> Use the **Overview** workspace to quickly verify your total employee headcount and net payroll before opening the register to make adjustments.

## Inspecting Employee Payslips

The **Payslips** workspace provides a card-based layout for every employee registered for the active period. Each card displays the employee's name, employee number, and avatar initials.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Payslips tab">
Navigate to your active payroll run and click on the **Payslips** workspace tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Select an employee">
Browse or search through the employee cards and click on any employee to open their detailed payslip view.
</Step>
<Step title="Review earnings and deductions">
In the modal that appears, inspect the breakdown of basic salary, allowances, overtime, PAYE, pension contributions, and total net pay.
</Step>
<Step title="Export the record">
Click **Export Payslip** to download a copy for your records or distribute it to the employee.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Tracking Payroll History

The **History** workspace keeps a complete record of all previous payroll runs. For each past period, you can quickly view:

- **Period identifier** and processing status (**Draft**, **Processing**, **Processed**, or **Paid**).
- **Employee count** and the exact date the payroll was processed.
- **Financial breakdown** including Gross Pay, Total Deductions, and Net Pay totals.

> [!NOTE]
> Historical records are read-only to ensure financial audit integrity. If you need to make changes to a finalized period, consult your system administrator regarding adjustment runs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="Does registering an employee automatically add them to payroll?">
No. Registering an employee in the system adds them to your directory pool, but you must explicitly select them for the active payroll period before calculations begin.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Can I export all employee payslips at once?">
Yes. In the **Payslips** workspace, click the **Export All** button at the top right to generate a bulk export of all employee payslips for the current period.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="How are statutory deductions calculated?">
Statutory deductions like PAYE, pension, and health insurance are automatically computed based on the employee's earnings profile and configured tax tables. You can review individual calculations inside each employee's payslip modal.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
