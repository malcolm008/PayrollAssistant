Get familiar with the core layout shell, collapsible sidebars, and navigation options designed to help you move smoothly through your workspace.

The application layout is structured around a flexible shell that adapts to your screen size, putting your main workspace, payroll details, and system settings just a click away.

## The Layout Shell

The application interface is divided into three main navigational areas:

1. **The Sidebar** — Houses your primary workspace links, collapsible submenus (like Payroll), and system settings.
2. **The Top Bar** — Provides a persistent header area tracking your active workspace view.
3. **The Mobile Bottom Tab Bar** — Offers quick touch navigation when you are viewing the app on smaller screens or mobile devices.

## Using the Collapsible Sidebar

The sidebar is your main compass for moving through the app. Depending on your preference and screen space, you can expand or collapse it to maximize your working area.

### Collapsing and Expanding

To toggle the sidebar width between full view (`250px`) and icon-only view (`76px`), click the **Collapse sidebar** button located on the right edge of the sidebar panel.

### Navigation Sections

The sidebar splits your navigation into two intuitive categories:

| Section | Available Links | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Dashboard, Employees, Attendance, Payroll, Advances, Reports, Terminations | Core day-to-day management tools for your organization. |
| **System** | Settings | Configuration options for your administrative environment. |

### Exploring the Payroll Submenu

The **Payroll** section includes a collapsible submenu for deeper tasks. When you are on any payroll-related route, this menu expands automatically. You can also click the dropdown arrow next to **Payroll** to reveal specific views:

- **Payroll Period** — Manage active schedules and periods.
- **Payroll Registration** — Handle new registration items.
- **Payroll Input** — Enter time and payment adjustments.
- **Payroll Register** — Review summary registers.
- **Payslips** — Access individual staff payslips.
- **Payroll History** — Look back at previous cycles.
