Keeping your business profile up to date ensures that your invoices, official reports, and customer communications always display the correct contact details and legal identity. 

## Managing Your Business Identity

Your company information is split into three main categories: identity details, location addresses, and communication channels. Keeping these fields current helps maintain professional consistency across your entire workspace.

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Company Settings">
From your main dashboard, go to **Settings** and select **Company Information** to open the details editor.
</Step>
<Step title="Update your details">
Review and update the fields across your identity, address, and contact sections as needed. Required fields, such as your **Company Name**, are marked with an asterisk.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your changes">
Click the **Save Changes** button at the top or bottom of the page to apply your updates instantly.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!TIP]
> You don't have to wait for a success notification to know your changes went through—a confirmation banner will appear at the top of the screen as soon as your updates are successfully saved.

## Overview of Company Fields

Here is a quick reference guide to the information you can configure for your organization:

| Section | Field Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Company Identity** | **Company Name** *(Required)* | The official name of your organization used across documents. |
| | **Incorporated As** | Your legal business structure (e.g., Limited Company, LLC, Sole Proprietorship). |
| | **Nature of Business** | A brief summary describing what your company does. |
| **Address Information** | **Address** | Your postal or P.O. Box address for mail delivery. |
| | **Physical Address** | Your street address, building name, or physical office location. |
| **Contact Information** | **Phone & Fax** | Primary telephone and fax numbers for reaching your offices. |
| | **Email Address** | The primary email address displayed on official documents and reports. |
| | **Website Link** | Your organization's main web address. |

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="Who can view or edit company information?">
Typically, only users with Administrator or Owner roles have the permissions necessary to update company-wide settings and identity details. If you cannot edit these fields, check with your workspace administrator.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Where do these details appear?">
Once saved, your company name, address, and contact details automatically populate your generated invoices, client-facing reports, and official workspace communications.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
