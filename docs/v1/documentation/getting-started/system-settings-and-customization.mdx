Customize your application experience and tailor your workspace to fit your team's needs. You can easily adjust interface themes and manage global system settings to keep everything running smoothly.

## Managing Your Appearance Settings

Personalize how your workspace looks by choosing a visual theme that suits your working environment and reduces eye strain.

## Available Themes

You can switch between two built-in themes at any time:

| Theme Name | Description | Best Suited For |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark** | The default interface designed with dark surfaces. | Low-light environments and reducing eye strain. |
| **Light** | A clean, bright interface paired with gold accents. | Well-lit offices and daytime working conditions. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to the **Settings** page from your main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Appearance section">
    Find the **Appearance** panel, which is expanded by default to display theme choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your preferred theme">
    Click on either the **Dark** or **Light** theme card to apply it instantly. A checkmark will appear on your active selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>
