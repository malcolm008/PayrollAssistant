Generate, preview, and export comprehensive payroll, attendance, and employee reports to keep your organization running smoothly and on budget.

## Overview of Reporting

The **Reports** dashboard gives you a centralized place to monitor organizational metrics, review department breakdowns, and download records for any active pay period. 

At the top of the dashboard, you will find summary cards tracking key totals:

- **Total Reports:** The complete library of reports available in your organization.
- **Payroll:** Reports focused on salaries, deductions, net pay, and disbursements.
- **Attendance:** Time-tracking records, leave summaries, and punctuality logs.
- **Employee:** Staff headcounts, department allocations, and personnel changes.

---

## Generating a Payroll Report

Follow these steps to generate, preview, and export a payroll report for any given month.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the reporting period">
Use the calendar dropdown in the top control bar to pick your target reporting period (for example, **September 2026**).
</Step>
<Step title="Filter by category">
Click the **Payroll** category button to narrow down the report list so only payroll-related documents are visible.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview the report">
Find the specific payroll report you need (such as the monthly disbursement summary) and click **Preview** to inspect the data table, employee counts, and department totals.
</Step>
<Step title="Export or print">
Once you are happy with the preview, click **Export** to download a copy or **Print** a physical record directly from your browser.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!TIP]
> You can use the search bar at the top of the reports list to quickly jump to a specific report title by keyword.

## Understanding Report Data

When you preview a payroll report, the assistant structures the information into an easy-to-read summary table. Below is an example of what you will see in a standard monthly payroll report:

| Employee | Department | Status | Amount |
|----------|------------|--------|--------|
| John Mwangi | Finance | Complete | TZS 2,420,000 |
| Sarah Johnson | Human Resources | Complete | TZS 3,100,000 |
| Michael Smith | Information Technology | Complete | TZS 4,300,000 |
| Amina Hassan | Administration | Pending | TZS 1,850,000 |

> [!NOTE]
> Records marked as **Pending** indicate that disbursement approval is still in progress for that particular employee or department.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="Can I generate reports for past months?">
Yes. The period selector retains historical data for all closed pay periods. Simply choose the month and year you wish to review from the dropdown menu.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="What formats are available when exporting?">
Reports can be exported for spreadsheet analysis or printed directly. If you need a specific format not currently shown, contact your system administrator.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Why is a report showing zero records?">
If a report returns no results, ensure that your selected reporting period matches the dates when payroll was processed for those departments.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
