Welcome to the Reports and Analytics center! Here, you can easily analyze overview metrics, review detailed payroll registers, check attendance summaries, and preview reports before exporting them.

## Available Report Categories

Reports are organized into categories to help you quickly find the exact information you need. You can filter reports by selecting any of the available categories:

| Category | Description | Common Frequency |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Payroll** | Detailed earnings, deductions, gross/net pay, and payslip data. | Monthly |
| **Attendance** | Presence, absence tracking, working hours, and overtime records. | Daily / Monthly |
| **Employees** | Complete staff listings, active statuses, and department breakdowns. | On Demand / Monthly |
| **Management** | High-level cost analyses across departments and reporting periods. | Monthly |

> [!TIP]
> Use the search bar at the top of the **Reports** page to quickly locate a specific report by name or description without having to browse through categories.

## Viewing and Previewing Reports

When you select a report, you can open a report preview modal to inspect the data before printing or downloading. 

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Reports">
Click on **Reports** in your main navigation menu to view the complete library of available reports.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a Report">
Browse by category or use the search bar, then click on the report card you wish to view (such as the **Payroll Register** or **Daily Attendance**).
</Step>
<Step title="Inspect the Preview Modal">
A modal window will open displaying the report title, category, and formatted data preview for your selected date range or period.
</Step>
<Step title="Export or Print">
Use the built-in action buttons in the preview header to download your report file or send it directly to your printer.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="How often are payroll reports updated?">
Most payroll reports, including the Payroll Register and Payroll Summary, are generated on a monthly basis, reflecting the completed payroll periods.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Can I customize the date range for attendance summaries?">
Yes! When opening attendance reports like Daily Attendance or Absence Reports, you can adjust the reporting period filters to view custom date ranges.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="What file formats are available when downloading reports?">
Depending on your system configuration, reports can typically be downloaded in standard formats such as CSV, Excel, or PDF for easy sharing and archiving.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
