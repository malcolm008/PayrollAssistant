Keep track of your organization's departmental spending and financial allocations efficiently using interactive tables, form modals, and deletion workflows.

Cost centers help you categorize financial operations by department, project, or location, making it easier to monitor budgets and analyze expenditures.

## Understanding the Cost Centers Dashboard

At the top of the **Cost Centers** page, you'll find summary cards that give you an instant snapshot of your organizational setup:

| Summary Card | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Total Cost Centers** | The overall count of active and inactive cost centers configured in the system. |
| **Active** | Cost centers currently available for new financial allocations and reporting. |
| **Inactive** | Cost centers that are archived or disabled; they cannot receive new expense allocations. |

---

## Managing Cost Centers

You can easily create, update, and organize your cost centers directly from the dashboard using the interactive table and modals.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create a new cost center">
Click the **New Cost Center** button in the top right corner to open the creation form modal.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the details">
Enter a unique **Cost Center Code** (such as `CC-001`), a descriptive **Name** (such as `Human Resources`), and optionally a detailed description of its purpose.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the status">
Check or uncheck the **Active Cost Center** toggle to determine whether it can immediately receive new expense allocations.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your entry">
Click **Create Cost Center** to save the record to your organization's directory.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!TIP]
> Cost center codes must be unique across your organization. If you try to save a code that already exists, the system will prompt you to choose a different one.

---

## Searching and Filtering

As your organization grows, you can quickly find specific cost centers using the built-in search bar and filter buttons:

- **Search Bar**: Type any part of a cost center code, name, or description to instantly filter the list.
- **Status Filters**: Switch between **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** views to narrow down your list based on operational status.

---

## Editing and Deleting Cost Centers

You can modify any existing cost center by clicking the **Edit** icon in the actions column of the table. Update any necessary fields in the modal and click **Save Changes**.

To remove a cost center, click the **Delete** icon. A confirmation dialog will appear to ensure you want to remove the record permanently.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting a cost center is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure you no longer need the cost center or its historical association before confirming deletion.
