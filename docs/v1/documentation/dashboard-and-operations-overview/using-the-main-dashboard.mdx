Welcome to the main dashboard guide. This overview helps you navigate your operations, manage payroll periods, and access essential tools from a single centralized interface.

## Understanding the Dashboard Layout

The main dashboard is organized into logical sections designed to keep your HR and payroll tasks running smoothly. At the top of the screen, you can always view your active **Payroll Period** (such as September 2026) to ensure you are working within the correct timeframe.

The workspace is divided into two main areas:
1. **Input Operations & System Administration (Left Grid):** Your day-to-day setup tools, including Cost Centers, GL Accounts, Account Allocation, Leave, Payroll Configuration, Income Tax Parameters, System Users, and Payment Variables.
2. **Payroll Operations (Right Rail):** Bulk and statutory actions like Global and Individual Increments, Pension Receipts, and Payslip Broadcasts.

```flowchart TD
    A[Main Dashboard] --> B[Input Operations]
    A --> C[System Administration]
    A --> D[Payroll Operations Rail]
    B --> E[Cost Centers and GL Accounts]
    B --> F[Leave and Configuration]
    C --> G[Tax and User Management]
    D --> H[Increments and Broadcasts]
```

## Managing Payroll Increments

When you need to adjust salaries—whether across the entire company or for a single staff member—you can use the dedicated increment modals found in the right-hand payroll rail.

<Steps>
<Step title="Select an Increment Type">
Choose **Global Increment** for bulk updates or **Individual Increment** to adjust a single employee's record.
</Step>
<Step title="Review Employee Data">
The modal will display relevant employee details such as their department, current status, and basic salary.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply Adjustments">
Enter your increment details, preview the changes, and click **Apply** to save your updates to the system.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!NOTE]
> System status indicators at the bottom of the page ensure you always know when the platform is fully operational. A green indicator light confirms that your payroll management system is connected and running normally.
