Configuring your payroll elements correctly ensures that advances, allowances, deductions, loans, and banking options calculate smoothly for every pay run. This guide walks you through setting up and organizing these essential financial categories in your management panels.

## Overview of Payroll Elements

The **Payroll Configuration** panel brings together the primary financial building blocks for your organization. Keeping these types structured helps maintain accurate payroll records and streamlines employee self-service requests.

| Category | Icon / Element | What it manages |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Advance Types** | `WalletCards` / `CreditCard` | Short-term salary advances requested by employees before payday. |
| **Allowance Types** | `CircleDollarSign` / `BadgePercent` | Recurring or one-off additions like housing, transport, or meal stipends. |
| **Deduction Types** | `ShieldCheck` / `BanknoteArrowDown` | Standardized salary subtractions like union dues, insurance, or penalties. |
| **Leave Types** | `CalendarDays` / `ClockFading` | Paid or unpaid time-off categories that affect payroll calculations. |
| **Loan Types** | `Banknote` / `BanknoteArrowUp` | Long-term company loans subject to installment-based repayments. |

---

## Navigating to Payroll Settings

You can access and customize all payroll and banking elements directly from your main settings dashboard.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Settings">
Navigate to the main **Settings** page from your dashboard navigation menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Locate Payroll Configuration">
Find the **Payroll Configuration** section and click it to expand the list of available categories.
</Step>
<Step title="Select an Element Type">
Click on the specific element type you wish to configure—such as **Advance Types**, **Allowance Types**, **Deduction Types**, **Leave Types**, or **Loan Types**—to open its dedicated management modal.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!TIP]
> You can also access quick setup panels directly from your primary payroll dashboard shortcuts if your role has administrative privileges.

---

## Setting Up Banking and Payment Options

In addition to salary elements, setting up your organization's banking preferences ensures seamless disbursement during pay runs.

1. Go to the **Banking** section in your settings panel.
2. Select **Banks** to add or manage your corporate banking partners.
3. Choose **Payment Methods** to configure how funds are transferred to employees (such as direct bank deposit, checks, or digital wallets).

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="Can I configure multiple payment methods for different employees?">
Yes! You can assign specific payment methods to individual employee profiles in their employee settings, allowing flexibility for staff who prefer direct deposit versus alternative payout methods.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="What should I do if a bank transfer fails?">
First, verify the employee's banking details under their profile. Then, check the **Payment Methods** configuration to ensure the bank code and routing details match your financial institution's requirements.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
