Managing employee financial advances is a straightforward way to support your team while keeping company finances transparent and organized. This guide shows you how to track advance records, monitor key financial metrics, and filter your register to find exactly what you need.

## Overview of Metric Calculations

At the top of the Advances page, you'll find four summary metric cards that give you an instant snapshot of your organization's financial health regarding advances:

| Metric Card | What It Measures | How It's Calculated |
|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Total Advances** | The cumulative sum of all advance requests in the system. | The total amount requested across all records. |
| **Outstanding** | The remaining balance that still needs to be recovered from employees. | Total Advanced minus Total Recovered. |
| **Recovered** | The total amount successfully collected back from employees so far. | The sum of all repayments received. |
| **Pending Approval** | The number of advance requests currently waiting for administrative review. | Count of records with the status "Pending Approval". |

> [!TIP]
> Keep an eye on the **Outstanding** balance to ensure repayments are progressing smoothly alongside your regular payroll cycles.

## Filtering and Searching the Advance Register

The Advance Register lists every financial advance request and allows you to quickly sort through records as your team grows. 

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to the Register">
Click on the **Register** tab at the top of the Advances module to view the complete list of records.
</Step>
<Step title="Search for Specific Records">
Use the **Search** bar to look up requests by employee name, employee number, department, or advance ID.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply Status Filters">
Use the **Status** dropdown to isolate requests by their current lifecycle stage—such as *Pending Approval*, *Approved*, *Paid*, or *Fully Recovered*.
</Step>
<Step title="Filter by Advance Type">
Use the **Advance Type** dropdown to view specific categories of financial support your company offers.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Advance Statuses

Every advance request moves through a series of stages. Here is what each status means for your workflow:

- **Draft**: The advance request has been created but not yet submitted for review.
- **Pending Approval**: The request is waiting for a manager or finance administrator to approve or reject it.
- **Approved**: The request has been approved and is ready to be disbursed.
- **Paid**: The funds have been disbursed to the employee.
- **Partially Recovered**: Some repayments have been made, but an outstanding balance remains.
- **Fully Recovered**: The advance has been completely repaid.
- **Rejected**: The request was reviewed and declined.
- **Cancelled**: The request was cancelled before completion.

> [!NOTE]
> You can click on any advance ID in the register to view detailed repayment histories and activity logs for that specific employee.
