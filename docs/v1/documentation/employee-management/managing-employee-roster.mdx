The Employee Roster is your central hub for organizing staff records, tracking department groupings, managing salary breakdowns, and maintaining up-to-date contact details. 

## Overview of the Employee Directory

When you open the **Employees** page, you'll see a split view layout:
- **Left Sidebar (Roster):** A searchable list of all team members grouped by department and sorted by surname. It includes quick status badges and filter controls.
- **Right Pane (Detail View):** A comprehensive record for whichever employee is currently selected, showing everything from pay structures to statutory details.

> [!TIP]
> You can easily switch between the employee roster and training courses using the view tabs at the top of the page.

---

## Adding and Managing Employees

Adding a new team member is straightforward. The system automatically generates unique employee numbers (such as `EMP-0001`) to prevent duplicates.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the add employee form">
Click the **Add employee** button located at the top of the left sidebar.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter personal and job details">
Fill in the required fields in the modal, including the employee's name, department, job title, and employment type.
</Step>
<Step title="Configure pay and benefits">
Add basic salary information, allowances, and any applicable statutory exemptions (such as SDL exemption or paid overtime).
</Step>
<Step title="Save the record">
Submit the form to add the employee to the roster. They will automatically appear under their respective department group.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Filtering and Searching the Roster

With a growing team, finding a specific record is quick using the built-in search and filter controls.

- **Search Bar:** Type any part of an employee's name, role, employee number, department, or cost code to filter instantly.
- **Status Filters:** Toggle between **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** staff members.
- **Department & Employment Type Selectors:** Narrow down your list to specific departments or contract types using the dropdown filters.

> [!NOTE]
> If your search or filters return no results, click **Clear filters** to reset the view immediately.

## Employee Record Breakdown

When you select an employee from the roster, their detailed profile is organized into logical sections:

| Section | What it includes |
|---------|------------------|
| **Pay** | Basic salary, itemized allowances, total compensation, paid overtime status, and leave entitlement days. |
| **Contact** | Email address, phone number, and residential location. |
| **Statutory and personal** | National ID number, TIN number, WCF number, gender, and date of birth. |
| **Work and housing** | Cost code, currency, company housing status, and rented house rent amounts. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion>
<AccordionTab title="What happens when I mark an employee as inactive?">
Inactive employees remain in the database for record-keeping and historical payroll purposes, but they are visually dimmed in the sidebar and separated from active staff views.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="Can I undo deleting an employee?">
No. Deleting an employee removes their record permanently. The system will prompt you for confirmation before completing this action.
</AccordionTab>
<AccordionTab title="How are employees sorted in the roster?">
Employees are automatically grouped alphabetically by department, and individuals within each department are sorted alphabetically by their surname.
</AccordionTab>
</Accordion>
