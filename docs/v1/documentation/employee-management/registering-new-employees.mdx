Onboarding new personnel and managing active payroll participation is straightforward using employee registration modals and workspace tabs. This guide walks you through navigating the workspace and registering employees for upcoming pay periods.

## Getting Started with the Payroll Workspace

When you open the **Payroll** section, you are greeted by an overview dashboard that summarizes active employee counts, net payroll totals, and quick-launch tools. 

Use the workspace tabs along the top to switch between different management views:

| Tab | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Overview** | Launches the registration modal and displays current period summaries. |
| **Allowances** | Manages earnings components and additional pay items. |
| **Deductions** | Tracks statutory and voluntary deductions for the period. |
| **Overtime** | Reviews overtime hours and calculated overtime pay. |
| **Payslips** | Previews and exports individual employee payslips. |
| **History** | Reviews previous pay periods, totals, and historical statuses. |

---

## Registering Employees for a Pay Period

To include or update personnel for an upcoming payroll run, you will use the **Payroll Register** launcher. Registration determines who is evaluated for earnings, deductions, and disbursements in the current cycle.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Payroll Workspace">
Navigate to the **Payroll** page from your main navigation menu and ensure you are on the **Overview** tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Launch the Registration Modal">
Click the **Open Register** button located in the Payroll Register summary card to open the employee selection modal.
</Step>
<Step title="Filter and Search Personnel">
Use the search bar to look up specific employees by name, employee number, or job position. You can also filter the list by department using the dropdown menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Select Employees">
Check the box next to each employee you wish to include in the current pay period. You can also use the select-all feature to include all visible personnel in your filtered view.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm Selection">
Save your changes to update the active payroll roster.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!NOTE]
> Registering an employee adds them to the active payroll run for the selected period, but it does not modify their permanent master profile or contract details.

## Managing Payroll Components

Once your employees are registered, you can refine their compensation details by navigating to the corresponding workspace tabs:

- **Allowances**: Add recurring or one-time earnings components.
- **Deductions**: Review standard tax withholdings, insurance contributions, or custom deductions.
- **Overtime**: Monitor accumulated overtime hours and verify calculated pay multipliers.

<Card title="Need to preview individual payslips?" icon="pi pi-file" href="#payslips">
Switch to the **Payslips** tab to review generated breakdowns for any registered employee before final disbursement.
</Card>
